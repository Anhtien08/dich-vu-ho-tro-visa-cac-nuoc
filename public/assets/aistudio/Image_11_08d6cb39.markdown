## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an aura of institutional credibility, procedural efficiency, and welcoming accessibility for premium Vietnamese visa and global travel consultancy. Balancing governmental rigor with modern hospitality, it reassures international travelers, business executives, and expatriates navigating complex immigration requirements.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modernism** with purposeful **Tactile Realism**:
- **Structured Trust:** Deep oceanic navy anchors authority and institutional backing, avoiding bureaucratic coldness through energetic warm accents.
- **High-Velocity Clarity:** High-conversion saffron-orange highlights decisive interactive paths, critical CTAs, and streamlined operational steps.
- **Pristine Whitespace & Micro-Borders:** Crisp, low-contrast slate borders and cool porcelain surfaces evoke the tactile precision of biometric passport folios, official documentation, and executive airport lounges.
- **Transparency & Compliance:** Clear typography hierarchies and verified credential badges cultivate trust without relying on hyperbolic marketing claims.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a deliberate balance of authoritative cool tones and high-conversion warm accents:

- **Primary Navy Blue (`#103B73`):** The backbone of the brand. Used for major headers, navigation bar backgrounds, hero grounding zones, primary structural anchors, and official insignia.
- **Secondary Blue (`#2478C9`):** Represents speed, technology, and clarity. Used for active tabs, dynamic step indicators, verified trust markers, and secondary action highlights.
- **Tertiary High-Conversion Orange (`#F58220`):** The conversion engine. Reserved strictly for primary transactional triggers, quick hotline badges, urgent status notices, and direct contact CTAs. It must never be diluted on non-actionable decorative surfaces.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals:**
  - `surface-canvas` (`#FFFFFF`): High-clarity backdrop for editorial forms and complex legal copy.
  - `surface-subtle` (`#F5F7FA`): Soft, cool foundation that provides structural grouping without harsh container lines.
  - `text-primary` (`#1E293B`) & `text-muted` (`#64748B`): Strict WCAG AAA compliant neutrals designed for legible legalese, visa requirements, and checklist readability.

## Typography
`Be Vietnam Pro` serves as the primary typographic family across all scales. Engineered specifically for complex diacritics in Vietnamese while retaining sleek international grotesque proportions, it ensures seamless bilingual clarity (Vietnamese and English).

- **Headings:** Heavy weights (`700`, `800`) with modest negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) maintain punchy editorial authority, establishing reliable hierarchies in consular step flows and country directories.
- **Body:** Standardized at `15px` and `17px` with deliberate vertical room (`1.6` to `1.65` relative leading) to prevent visual crowding across procedural visa conditions and multi-lingual requirements.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Uppercase micro-copy (`label-caps`) handles official classifications, visa processing categories (e.g., "E-VISA", "WORK PERMIT"), and timeline timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a disciplined **12-column responsive grid** bound within a maximum content container of `1240px`:

- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins. Hero sections, multi-step application maps, and country visual decks use asymmetric splits (e.g., 7:5 ratio for consultation lead capture).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters. Country matrices condense to 2-up displays; consultation forms expand to full-span structural cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` outer margins. Content aligns in unified vertical stacks. Crucial interactive components anchor to a fixed-position bottom conversion bar.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections step down through proportional increments: `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) within related component groupings, and `space-3xl` (`6rem`) between foundational service narratives.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is expressed through subtle atmospheric layering and calibrated tonal borders rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Background):** Base layer using `#FFFFFF` and `#F5F7FA` panels, separating topical sections via crisp `1px` borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Static Cards & Containers):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards resting on `#F5F7FA` backdrops, framed with `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient, diffused shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(16, 59, 115, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(16, 59, 115, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Popovers):** Elevated destination cards, active dropdowns, and form panels: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(16, 59, 115, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(16, 59, 115, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Sticky Navigation & Mobile Conversion Dock):** Frosted glassmorphism using `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` paired with `border-b: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` and `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 59, 115, 0.06)`.

## Shapes
The shape system operates at **Level 2 (Rounded)**:
- Standard buttons, input fields, accordion rows, and badges utilize an `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- Cards, visual image containers, consultation modal panels, and lead forms scale up to `16px` (`1rem`, `rounded-lg`).
- High-priority telephone badges, filter pills, and quick floating triggers use fully rounded pill profiles (`9999px`).
- Form elements maintain balanced geometric contours to echo secure, credentialed passport cards.

## Components

### 1. Navigation Header
- **Desktop:** Crisp white glass background with `border-subtle` bottom separator. Features brand logotype on the left, horizontal anchor links in `text-primary`, an emergency hotline indicator enclosed in a pill badge (`#2478C9` with soft `10%` fill), and the primary `#F58220` CTA ("Tư Vấn Ngay").
- **Mobile Conversion Bar:** Fixed bottom dock (`72px` height) with split actions: a primary telephone click-to-call icon button (`#103B73`) alongside a wide conversion button (`#F58220`) triggering a quick-consultation modal drawer.

### 2. Action Buttons & Chips
- **Primary Action (`CTA`):** `#F58220` background, pure white text, font weight `600`, `8px` radius. Hover states shift to an energized shade (`#E07010`) with `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary Action:** Ghost container with `#103B73` text, `1.5px` border in `#103B73`, or light `#2478C9` subtle fill (`rgba(36, 120, 201, 0.08)`).
- **Status & Requirement Chips:** Compact pill badges (`rounded-full`) in soft tint backgrounds (e.g., green tint for "99.2% Thành Công", light slate for "3 - 5 Ngày Làm Việc") accompanied by micro icons.

### 3. Consultation Lead Form
- **Form Surface:** Encapsulated in a `rounded-lg` container with pure white background, framed by `border-subtle` and Level 2 elevation.
- **Inputs:** Height `48px`, font size `15px`, `8px` border radius, `#F5F7FA` base fill transitioning to pure `#FFFFFF` on `:focus` with a dual ring: `1px solid #2478C9` and `3px outline rgba(36, 120, 201, 0.15)`.
- **Phone Prefix Validation:** Dedicated country code selector dock with integrated national flags preceding the telephone digit input.
- **Trust Footers:** Encrypted SSL seal icon and standard compliance statement in `body-sm` (`#64748B`) directly below the submission trigger.

### 4. Country Destination Travel Cards
- **Card Structure:** `rounded-lg` container with `overflow-hidden`. Top half features a 16:10 curated travel destination photo with an embedded country flag badge in the top-left corner.
- **Card Content:** Country name in `headline-sm`, followed by a 2-column key-value matrix (Processing Time, Validity, Success Rate) rendered in `body-sm`.
- **Card Footer:** Subtle horizontal divider leading to an inline text-link CTA ("Xem Chi Tiết") with animated chevron affordance.

### 5. 4-Step Progressive Timeline
- **Layout:** Horizontal 4-step row on desktop; connected vertical track on mobile.
- **Step Indicator:** Numbered circular nodes (`40px` diameter). Completed/Active steps feature deep `#103B73` fill with white numerals; connecting track lines transition from `#2478C9` to `#E2E8F0`.
- **Step Content:** Step title in `label-lg` (`#1E293B`) paired with a 2-line micro explanation in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### 6. Accordions & Customer Feedback
- **Accordion:** Clean bordered item containers with `8px` corner radius. Expanded headers display `#103B73` color with smooth 180-degree icon rotation.
- **Feedback Cards:** Compact blockquote containers with 5-star golden badge clusters, reviewer portrait, verification badge ("Đã đậu Visa"), and traveling destination flag.
